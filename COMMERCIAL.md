## 💼 Why Choose a Commercial License?

The **GNU Affero General Public License v3 (AGPLv3)** ensures that any company offering Envshield as a hosted network service (SaaS) or modifying it within proprietary backends must open-source their entire platform under the same license.

A **Commercial License** frees your organization from this requirement:
1. **Proprietary SaaS Exemption:** Embed, distribute, or host Envshield without being required to release your proprietary application source code.
2. **SOC 2 & ISO 27001 Certification:** Access to formal audit compliance certificates, cryptographically sealed verification logs, and audit assistance.
3. **Dedicated Enterprise Support:** Direct Slack / Teams escalation channel with core maintainers and guaranteed SLAs.
4. **Custom Integrations:** Priority support for custom Hardware Security Modules (HSM), Cloud KMS providers, and corporate identity providers (Okta, Azure AD, Ping).
